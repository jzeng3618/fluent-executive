# FluentExecutive - Gemini-Powered Business & Spoken English Coach

An intelligent, interactive workplace communication coaching suite powered by **Google Gemini**. Designed for professionals, engineering leads, product managers, and executives aiming to elevate their spoken fluency, diplomatic nuance, and business writing presence.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (Zero Setup Needed)

Because **FluentExecutive** runs directly in your modern web browser, you do **not** need to install Node.js, Python, or external dependencies.

### 1. Open the App
In your terminal, simply run:
```bash
open index.html
```
Or double-click `index.html` in Finder to launch it in **Google Chrome**, **Safari**, or **Microsoft Edge**.

### 2. Connect Your Gemini API Key
1. Click the **"Setup Gemini Key"** badge in the top-right header (or open the gear icon).
2. Enter your Gemini API Key (get a free key at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey)).
3. Click **"Test Connection"** to verify, then click **"Save Settings"**.
*(Your API key is stored safely in your browser's `localStorage` and never leaves your machine).*

## 🎯 What's Included in the Toolset

### ✍️ 1. Business Writing & Tone Refiner
- **Tone Matrix**: Switch easily between *Executive (BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front)*, *Diplomatic (softening pushback)*, *Assertive (firm deadlines)*, and *Warm/Collaborative*.
- **Audience Context**: Calibrate tone for C-suite directors, cross-functional peers, direct reports, or external clients.
- **Communication Breakdown**: Explains *why* the rewrite works better and teaches corporate politeness markers.
- **1-Click Vocabulary Upgrades**: Suggests high-leverage business idioms and power verbs with one-click saving to your phrase vault.

### 🎙️ 2. Interactive Voice Roleplay & Scenario Arena
- **Real-World Personas**:
  - *Sarah Jenkins (VP of Product)*: Negotiate aggressive deadlines and scope creep.
  - *David Miller (Director of Eng)*: Pitch your mid-year achievements and compensation review.
  - *Rachel Chen (Chief of Staff)*: Deliver a crisp 60-second status update on blockers.
  - *Dr. Marcus Vance (Enterprise Architect)*: Handle tough technical objections after a demo.
- **Natural Voice Dialogue**:
  - Click the **Microphone** button to speak your response aloud.
  - The AI speaks back using browser speech synthesis.
- **Comprehensive Debrief**: Click **"Finish & Get Debrief"** to get a structured scorecard on clarity, tact, and alternate executive phrases.

### ⏱️ 3. Speech Diagnostics & Impromptu Pitch Coach
- **Timed Speaking Challenges**: Practice 30s/60s elevator pitches and impromptu thinking.
- **Real-time Vitals**:
  - **Filler Word Detector**: Flags "um", "uh", "you know", "like", "basically", and "actually".
  - **Cadence & WPM Meter**: Measures your speaking rate against the optimal 130–155 WPM executive pace.
- **Framework Analysis**: Gemini evaluates whether you followed structured frameworks like **PREP** (Point, Reason, Example, Point) or **STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

### 📚 4. Executive Phrasebook & Idiom Vault
- Preloaded with high-impact corporate expressions (e.g., *"To piggyback on what..."*, *"Help me understand the rationale behind..."*, *"I want to be mindful of everyone's time..."*).
- Audio pronunciation buttons to hear native intonation.
- Categorized filters (*Diplomatic Pushback*, *Executive Summaries*, *Smooth Transitions*, *Negotiation*).
- Add custom phrases with one click from any module.

## 🔒 Privacy & Architecture
- **Pure Client-Side**: All API requests communicate directly between your browser and Google's official Gemini endpoint.
- **No Third-Party Telemetry**: Your audio and drafts are never recorded or forwarded to any third-party server.
